Everyone is talking about **AI Agents**.
But very few are actually **running them in production**.

LangChain just released their **State of Agent Engineering** report, and the results are telling:

- 57% of companies already have agents in production
- Quality & reliability are now bigger challenges than cost
- Observability is becoming essential
- Multi-model strategies are becoming the norm

## The big shift

We're moving from "**prompt engineering**" to "**agent engineering**."

And that changes everything.

Building AI agents today isn't just about calling an LLM anymore.
It's about building **systems**:

- Evaluation pipelines
- Memory & context management
- Tool orchestration
- Monitoring & debugging
- Reliability at scale

## The takeaway

AI agents are no longer experiments.
They're becoming **production infrastructure**.

If you're working in AI right now, this report is worth your time.
